How to play

Learn it in three steps.

Pick a starting dot

Touch any dot to begin. Tip: a dot with an odd number of lines is where your stroke starts or ends.

Trace every line

Drag along the lines. Each one lights up as you draw it and must be used exactly once.

Never lift your finger

Cover the whole picture in a single continuous stroke to clear the level.

Features

Everything in Strokevale.

🎯

600 levels

Starts gently with short tutorials and grows into real brain-teasers with more dots and crossing lines.

🖼️

200 pictures

Every 3rd level is a picture: a heart, a rocket, a castle, a turtle. 50 designs in 4 colour editions.

🔀

Tricky twists

Double lines must be drawn twice. One-way lines only go in the arrow's direction.

💡

Help when you need it

Undo, restart from a new dot, or use a hint to reveal the next few lines.

🪙

Earn coins

Finish levels to earn coins, with a bonus for a perfect stroke.

✨

Bright & satisfying

Glowing lines, sparkles, confetti and calm music. Light and dark themes.

⏳

No timers, no lives

Play at your own pace. There's no clock and nothing to lose.

📤

Share your art

Send your finished drawings to friends.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.
