Strokevale Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Strokevale ("the app") is a one-stroke drawing puzzle game published by Morning Ray Games. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, or any account information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

Your settings (such as the theme, sound and vibration), your level progress, stars, coins and collected pictures are stored only on your device (local storage). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The app shows ads (banner and rewarded video) provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to show personalized ads where permitted. Google acts as a third-party data processor/controller for this purpose. See Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Rewarded video ads are optional. You choose to watch one to get more coins or extra undos.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It collects app usage data (for example which levels are started and finished, how long they take, and whether hints, undos or rewarded videos are used), an app instance ID, your advertising ID, and device information such as model, OS version and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is not linked to your name or email. See Firebase privacy information.

Sharing

If you use the in-app share feature, the app creates an image of your finished drawing and passes it to the sharing option you choose through your device's share sheet. We do not receive this content.

We do not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
