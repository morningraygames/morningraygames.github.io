The studio

Puzzles that feel like the first light of the day.

We are a small independent studio that builds hyper-casual puzzle games for phones. Our games are designed for the gaps in your day: the coffee break, the bus ride, the five quiet minutes before bed.

Every game starts with one simple rule you can learn in seconds. From there we craft hundreds of levels that slowly stretch your thinking, with bright colours, satisfying feedback and calm music along the way.

No sign-ups, no pressure, no clutter. Just a good puzzle, whenever you want one.

Our promise

What every Morning Ray game gets right.

The same four principles go into each game we make.

Learn in seconds

One rule, one tap. A short tutorial and you're playing. Depth comes later, at your pace.

Calm by design

Soft colours, gentle sounds and sparkles when you win. Games that relax you, not rush you.

Private by default

No accounts and no sign-in. Your progress and settings stay on your own device.

Light & dark

Every game ships with both themes, so it looks great at noon and easy on the eyes at night.
