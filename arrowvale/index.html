How to play

Learn it in three steps.

Read the board

Each arrow points the way it will move. Look for the ones with a clear path to the edge.

Tap to slide

Tap an arrow and it glides off the board. Hit another arrow and you lose a life.

Clear everything

Remove every arrow to win. No mistakes earns you the full 3 stars.
